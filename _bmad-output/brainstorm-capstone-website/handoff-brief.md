# Capstone Team Website — Handoff Brief

## 1. Session summary
Goal: a website that converts cold-email company readers into a short call. Session framed the site as the convincer after a short personalized email: show a clear team identity, back capability with real proof, state simple working terms, and end on one easy entry point (a short call). Readers are busy company engineers, innovation staff, and founders who delete most student cold emails.

## 2. Positioning
- The team is the product: a directed, self-driving hardware team with a visible thesis, not a list of skills or a graduation-requirement group.
- Differentiator: human-centred design, backed by proof, capability first. Claim is capable engineers who also design around real operators and users — built things by hand plus evidence of method.
- Rule: original wording only. Do not reuse wording, structure labels, or lines from any example site. Take only the intent (identity → value → contact arc that earns a call).

## 3. Tagline decision
- Hero: Hardware first. Humans always. Your problem, our final year.
- Subline (adapted from candidate 1): Five SUTD seniors — hardware first, humans always — looking for one company problem worth our final year. (Wording still open to mutation.)
- Status: still open to mutation. Group may tighten the final wording, but keep the order: capability first, human-centred second, ask last.

## 4. Email vs website split
- Email personalizes: 1–2 example directions matched to that company so it feels tailored. Reader decides nothing in the email.
- Website universalizes: no fixed project list. A fixed list lets a reader conclude “they only want these” and leave.

1. Hook — open on the reader’s stakes or our claim; 1–2 lines, no throat-clearing.
2. Bet — hardware is the easy part, humans are the hard part; state the team thesis in plain terms.
3. Proof-as-method — 1–2 real stories plus visible method (observe → requirements → iterate/build); show we go on site before building.

5. Offer + ask — what working together looks like plus one low-friction ask: a short call next week. Contact method/link [fill in with details later].
- Cold-open choice left open: their problem vs our claim vs a proof moment. Suggestion from session: the email can already carry the “their problem” hook, so the site is free to open on the claim.
